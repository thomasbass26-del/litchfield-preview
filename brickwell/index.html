The house

The house is Southern Colonial in form: a two-story columned porch, broad brick steps, dark shutters, and mature planting. Inside, the ceilings vault, a free-floating spiral stair rises from the entry, and the millwork is custom throughout.

The kitchen was renovated by Prodigy Kitchens and Baths, with an expansive island and a built-in Miele coffee system. The main living room has a gas fireplace and opens to a second entertaining room the owners call the Men's Club: coffered ceilings, Sonos sound, and a wet bar with a wine refrigerator and ice maker. A study with built-ins, a dining room, a laundry room, and a second office round out the main level.

The primary suite has its own fireplace and a claw-foot tub. Three more bedrooms in the main house each have a private bath. Upstairs there is a library with built-ins. Over the three-car garage, the fifth bedroom and its full bath have their own entrance, so they work as a guest suite or an in-law suite. A mudroom sits between the garage and the house, and an elevator shaft, used for storage today, is already framed.

Outside, a 20 by 40 foot saltwater pool sits in a terrace, and the grounds run down to a private stocked pond with its own island, a fountain, and landscape lighting. The rear porch has a gas fireplace and a hot tub overlooking the pool. The basement is open space to finish as the owner likes.

The Reserve at Litchfield

The Reserve is gated, and Island Estates is the enclave within it. Ownership here carries access to Litchfield By The Sea's private beach and to the neighborhood marina and boat ramp. The Harbor Club, with its restaurant and pool, is a separate membership owners may join, and the community's private golf club, on a course designed by Greg Norman, is its own membership as well. Golf carts are allowed on the sidewalks that run through the community.

Pawleys Island's beaches and restaurants are minutes away on Highway 17. Waccamaw Elementary, Middle, and High School serve the address.
