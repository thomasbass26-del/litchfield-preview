Privacy on this scale is rare in Pawleys Island: nearly two acres behind a gate, mature trees, and a pond with its own island, minutes from the beach. This week, four homes are for sale in The Reserve. This is one.

What a day here looks like

  • Morning

    Coffee from the built-in Miele in a kitchen renovated by Prodigy Kitchens and Baths, at an island long enough for the whole house. The study, with its built-ins, is a few steps away for anyone who has to work.

  • Afternoon

    The pool is 20 by 40 feet and saltwater. Past it, the lawn runs down to the pond, where two chairs face the island. Upstairs, a library lined with built-ins for the hour when it is too hot to be outside.

  • Evening

    The room the owners call the Men's Club: coffered ceilings, a wet bar with a wine refrigerator and ice maker, Sonos through the walls. Then the rear porch, where a gas fireplace and a hot tub look out over the lit pool.

  • Night

    The primary suite has its own fireplace and a claw-foot tub. Three more bedrooms in the house each have a private bath, and the fifth bedroom, over the garage, has its own entrance, so guests come and go without passing through the house.

The Reserve at Litchfield

The Reserve is gated, and Island Estates is the enclave within it. Ownership here carries access to Litchfield By The Sea's private beach and to the neighborhood marina and boat ramp. The Harbor Club, with its restaurant and pool, is a separate membership owners may join, and the community's private golf club, on a course designed by Greg Norman, is its own membership as well. Golf carts are allowed on the sidewalks that run through the community.

Pawleys Island's beaches and restaurants are minutes away on Highway 17. Waccamaw Elementary, Middle, and High School serve the address.
