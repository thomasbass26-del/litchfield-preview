Jenna's coast

Murrells Inlet, Garden City, Surfside, and Myrtle Beach: the inlet and the creeks, the beach, the golf, and the city's shops and hospitals, with Pawleys Island and Georgetown a short drive south and Charleston a longer one.

How she works

  1. A conversation first

    What you are looking for, where, and on what timing. Most of it happens by telephone.

  2. A short list, not a long one

    Properties chosen against what you said, with the reasons for each, and the ones she would pass on.

  3. Showings on your schedule

    In person, or by video for buyers who are not yet on the coast.

  4. The firm behind her

    Offers, inspections, and closing run through the brokerage, with an office to call if she is unreachable.
